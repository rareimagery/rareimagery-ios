Existing code (CircleService.maxCircleSize) just shows a toast. Upgraded to an explicit at-cap state — frame the cap as a feature, not a limit.

Design notes

1Default segment

CircleTabView.swift:8 sets selectedSegment = .discover, so Discover is the landing segment — not My Circle. Mockup screen 1 reflects this. If you want first-launch users to land on My Circle when their circle is non-empty, that's a one-line change.

2The 24-cap counter

CircleService.maxCircleSize = 24 is invisible until you hit it (you get a toast). Surfacing the "8 of 24" counter in My Circle (screen 2) makes the limit feel intentional and adds gentle pressure to curate vs. accumulate — a value lever, not a constraint.

3"Send to Circle" placement

Inserted in FirstProductCompleteView between the X share and "Continue to Rare". This is the right moment — they've just made a thing, are at peak excitement, and the dopamine of getting instant Circle feedback is what makes the loop sticky.

4Quick actions over multi-select

90% of Sends will be "ask everyone" or "ask my favorites" — make those one-tap. Multi-select is the escape hatch for the long tail. The proposed code already hints at this; the mockup makes the visual hierarchy explicit.

5Note field below the list

Standard iOS pattern: input + primary CTA pinned to the bottom with a translucent bar (matches Messages, Mail). Auto-fills with "What do you think of this drop?" if left blank — friction-free but personalized.

6"NEW" pill — lifecycle

Drop the orange "NEW" pill on the Send to Circle CTA for the first 7 days post-launch, or until the user has tapped it once. Avoid leaving it on forever — it loses signal.

7What's intentionally NOT in the sheet

No notification-type picker (DM vs. tag vs. in-app feed) — pick one default behavior server-side and iterate. No public/private toggle — defaults to private Circle-only. Keep the surface tiny; the value is the closed loop, not the configurability.

8Backend shape

Suggest a single endpoint: POST /api/circle/share with { draftID, recipientIDs, note, intent: "feedback" | "promote" }. Fan-out to push + in-app notifications happens server-side so the client stays dumb. The proposed CircleNotification SwiftData model is right for the receiver-side cache.

Failure-handling notes (F1–F6)

F1Empty circle is the modal case

For week-1 users, everyone hits this — the Circle starts empty. The Send sheet must recognize zero members and inline-promote the Discover flow, not just disable Send. The "Share on X" escape keeps the path forward even if they don't want to build a Circle right now.

F2Never lose the draft

On send failure, preserve selection + note locally and queue for retry. Auto-retry on connectivity restored; manual Retry now for impatient users. The red "Send failed — Tap to retry" CTA must remain tappable; don't dismiss the sheet.

F3System-fail vs. user-fail

Distinguish "POST failed" from "this person blocked you on X" — they're different recovery paths and need different copy. System failures get Retry; user-graph failures get an explanation + dismiss. Don't blanket-blame either side.

F4Local-first sync

The existing persistAndReload() already saves locally before sync, which is the right call. Just elevate the error banner from a plain orange string to a real dismissable banner with explicit Retry — current implementation is correct but invisible.

F5Honest search failure copy

"X may be rate-limited" is a real reason searches fail and worth saying. Users blame themselves when systems are vague. Naming the third party shifts the right amount of trust.

F6Cap as feature, not limit

Frame "24 max" as a curation principle, not a paywall opportunity. Twitter taught us bigger-isn't-better with Circles. Don't ever introduce a "Pro tier — unlimited Circle" — it'd destroy the value prop.

★Things to design before launch

Not in these mockups but worth wireframing before ship: rate-limit on the share endpoint itself ("you've sent 3 in the last hour"), what the recipient sees on push tap when the draft was since deleted, and the explicit "block this sender" path so F3 isn't the only blocked-state UI.

⊕Telemetry to add

Each failure mockup is a counter we should track: circle.send.fail.network, circle.send.partial, circle.sync.fail, circle.search.fail, circle.cap.hit. If one spikes after launch, we know what to fix first.
